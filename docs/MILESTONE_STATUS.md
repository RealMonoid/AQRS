## M2-E23 to E25 Test Card: three E22 filters

**Authorization and sequence, 2026-11-14.** The owner's "Freigabe" approves
the revised standing research proposal and the final eight-hour notice and
December-onward lock. It explicitly starts these three questions, in order,
then derived research. Intake approximately 14:23 UTC; implementation/checks
estimate 45–90 minutes for the shared slice, not measured past E22 effort.
E22's recorded prior effort is a lower bound of 34m 07s, not 2.5 measured hours.
The eight-hour notice is cumulative; Governor limits stay fixed.

**Smallest decision-changing slice.** Reuse the 15 original monthly E22 ZIPs
and checksums offline, no network. Training 2024-09-01–2024-11-30 (91 days),
evaluation 2024-12-01–2025-11-30 (365 days, 17,520 targets). Keep E22 next-bar
direction, ties, constant controls and separately conditional 25-bps long/flat
economics. A whole year preserves the owner-fixed comparison and seasonal
coverage; subgroup/joint-gate power and independent effective counts are not
established. No data extension after results. No protected dates are opened.

Three predeclared single filters: `abs(close/open - 1)`;
completed-bar volume divided by the mean at the same UTC half-hour and weekday
in the preceding four weeks (`t-336`, `t-672`, `t-1008`, `t-1344`); and
`abs(2*taker_buy_base/volume - 1)` (zero-volume bars ineligible). Use each
feature's training-only 80th percentile (`quantile`, linear interpolation), no
search; select values strictly above it. Undefined features are in neither
group. Use only features available at signal close t for target t+1; group by
target UTC date. Calibrate on finite features at indices 0 through TRAIN-2,
with the first 1,344 volume-feature rows ineligible. The volume denominator
must be positive; E22 majority calibration remains separate. E22 occupies slot 1,
these questions occupy 2–4 of ten for the evaluation year.

**Pre-outcome E24 correction, owner-supplied review:** the initial unexecuted
24-hour denominator confounded recurring calendar volume with unusual volume.
A synthetic weekly/day seasonal series produced 860 selections, 62.79% in
the busiest six half-hours; matching prior weekly slots produced ratio 1 and
zero selections. No market outcome was inspected. [Hansen, Kim and Kimbrough,
v2 2021; published 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12142v2) documents day/week/
hour patterns on Binance and other venues. Four prior weeks is a fixed design
choice, not an optimized or literature-proven optimum; changing seasonality
and other confounding remain possible. The discarded draft is not a market
attempt. Verify recurring-pattern rejection and injected anomalies in tests.

**Statistical design, fixed before implementation/outcomes.**
Compare selected reversal accuracy with unselected accuracy, and selected
reversal with both constant controls on the same selected opportunities.
Daily numerator/count influence vectors retain all calendar days, including
zero-selection days; apply existing Bartlett/Newey–West HAC at 7/14 days.
E24 also requires the fixed 28-day sensitivity because weekly inputs are shared;
none of these bandwidths proves complete dependence correction. All six bounds
(nine for E24) must exceed zero at nominal one-sided alpha 0.005 before
the cost stage. This conservative ten-slot screen does not correct unknown
adaptive E22 exposure and is never a confirmation claim. Require at least 180
days and all 12 evaluation months with selected cases, and the same coverage
for unselected cases; this is a descriptive calendar-coverage floor, not a
power guarantee. Center ratio intervals on the estimated ratio/contrast,
using the HAC standard error of its zero-mean influence vector and z(0.995).
Retain unfiltered E22 as an economic comparator on the full opportunity grid.
Independent design review accepted these contrasts for conditional description,
not causality or independently calibrated significance. Reuse [Newey–West
(1986/1987)](https://www.nber.org/papers/t0055) for asymptotic HAC and [Dwork
et al. (2015)](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/hash/bad5f33780c42f2588878a9d07405083-Abstract.html)
for the adaptive-reuse limitation; reviewed 2026-11-14. Neither establishes
finite-sample validity or power for these filters. No new literature framework.
Report selected cases, days, comparator and intervals even on failure; zero
economic trades never substitute for a prediction measurement. An estimated
positive net result is an immediate owner decision for that candidate.

**Resources and proof.** One isolated offline worker per question, two
calibrations (threshold and training-majority control), zero model fits,
resamples or automatic retries; max 120s worker/300s campaign, one concurrent
worker, 4096 MiB observed RSS under existing tighter-limit admission. Estimate
under 15s and 256 MiB per worker from the small fixed 21,888-row input; use the
permitted upper runtime bound for admission. No new scheduler or dependency.
Risk screen: [focused FTA](RISK_REGISTER.md#m2-e23-to-e25-filter-research-and-future-data-lock).
Before outcomes: causal/train-only/ratio-HAC/null/planted/holdout/Governor/
journal checks, independent source review, native Windows and non-root Linux
synthetic verification, then published source-bound registration. Acceptance
is correctly counted interpretable research, not a positive market result.
